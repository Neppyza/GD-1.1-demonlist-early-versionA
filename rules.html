Rules

A fair list starts with accurate information and respectful players.

CommunityLevel submissionsCompletionsAccounts and privacy

Community

  1. No swearing, slurs, harassment, or hateful language.
  2. No sexual, violent, or otherwise inappropriate names, images, links, or content.
  3. No spam, impersonation, cheating, or fake completion proof.
  4. Respect other players. Staff review submissions and may reject content that breaks these rules.

Community agreement version: 2026-10-05.

Level submissions

  • Use respectful, appropriate player and level names. Inappropriate names or content will be rejected.
  • Include a valid HTTPS level page link and a video link showing the completion.
  • Enter the creator and verifier accurately. A verifier is the first player to complete and verify the level.
  • Only submit a level you have permission to share. Staff decide its placement and points.

Submissions remain private while pending. Only authorized administrators can publish official list entries.

Completions and player rankings

Victors are players with approved 100% completion records. Staff check proof before approving a record.

Each approved level awards its current points once per player. Duplicate records do not award additional points. The list, Records, and Players all use the same approved records.

Accounts and privacy

The Demonlist uses Google sign-in through Firebase. Your first successful Google sign-in creates your account. Your private profile and Google email are not part of the public player rankings.

A signed-in account is required to accept these rules, save a profile, or send a level submission. Staff access requires permissions granted by the project owner.
