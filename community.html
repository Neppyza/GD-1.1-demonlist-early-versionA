Keep the community welcoming

  • No swearing, slurs, harassment, or hateful language.
  • No sexual, violent, or otherwise inappropriate names, images, links, or content.
  • No spam, impersonation, cheating, or fake completion proof.
  • Respect other players. Staff review submissions and may reject content that breaks these rules.

Your private profile

Submit a level

Submissions stay private while pending review. The list team checks every level and name before publishing.

Submission rules
  • Use respectful, appropriate player and level names. Inappropriate names or content will be rejected.
  • Include the level page link and a video link showing the completion.
  • Enter the level creator and verifier accurately. A verifier is the first player to complete and verify the level. Victors are players with approved 100% completion records.
  • Only submit a level you have permission to share. Staff decides its placement and points.
